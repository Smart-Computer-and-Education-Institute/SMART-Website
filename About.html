Dear Partners,

I am the founder of Smart Computer and Education Institute, established with the goal of providing practical and accessible computer education. Our aim is to help students and learners develop useful digital skills for their education, careers and everyday lives.

We offer computer training and educational programs focused on practical learning, personal guidance and skill development. Our friendly learning environment and dedicated support help every learner gain confidence and become more capable.

Our Story

At Smart Computer & Education Institute, we believe education should be practical, affordable, and career-focused. For over 12 years, we have been helping students build confidence through quality computer training, language classes, and skill-based courses.

Our Mission

We believe education changes lives. Our mission is to make quality computer and language education accessible to everyone by providing practical training, experienced instructors, and real-world learning that prepares students for academic and career success.

Our Vision

To be the leading skill-development hub in Jhapa, empowering individuals with cutting-edge digital literacy, vocational proficiency, and global career opportunities.

Why Choose Us

With 12+ years of teaching experience, flexible shift timings, hands-on lab access, and dedicated job placement assistance, we ensure every student transforms learning into success.
